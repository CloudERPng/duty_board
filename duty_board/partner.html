How it works

Register the lead first. A lead counts as yours when you register it here before Xlevel is in contact with the company — the timestamp on the registration settles it. A registered lead lapses after 12 months if it hasn't converted.
Your cut. Your rate applies to Implementation and Subscription fees your clients pay, net of VAT — not hardware, message fees or change requests. The rate in force on the day a client converts is locked to that client for its lifetime.
Earned when paid. Commission accrues when the client's payment reaches Xlevel, not when the invoice is issued. Each client's page shows every invoice, what has been paid and when.
Statements. On the first working days of each month you get a statement for the previous month: every payment your clients made, the commission on each, withholding tax deducted, and the net Xlevel pays you. Credit notes reverse commission in the month they are issued.
